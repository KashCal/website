# Navigation & shortcuts

This page covers the navigation drawer, the account hub, home-screen shortcuts, search
and syncing on demand.

## The top bar

- The menu icon opens the navigation drawer.
- The calendar icon showing today's date, next to the menu icon, jumps to today.
- The magnifier opens search.
- The round avatar opens the account hub.

When you're offline and have a synced account, a cloud-off icon shows beside the
magnifier.

## The navigation drawer

Swipe in from the left edge of the screen, or tap the menu icon, to open the drawer.
From here you can:

- Switch views: Insights, then Agenda, Day, 3 Days, Week, Month, Month (Full) and
  Year.
- Show or hide calendars. Each calendar has a checkbox and a color dot. Your
  calendars are grouped by account. **Contacts** (birthdays and anniversaries) and
  **Device calendars** (calendars from other apps on your phone) have their own
  sections. The device section appears only after you turn on
  [device calendars](../sync/device-calendars.md).
- **Settings**: opens Settings.
- **Help & docs**: opens the Get help section of this site in your browser.

## The account hub

Tap the round avatar in the top-right corner of any calendar view to open the account
hub. It's a full-screen page for your accounts, the app's look and your privacy
settings.

At the top is a large avatar. Tap it, or the pencil on it, to set up to two initials.
Until you do, it shows a person outline.

The hub has these parts:

- **Accounts & Settings**, an outlined button under the avatar that opens Settings, where
  you connect accounts and calendars.
- **Make it yours**, where you personalize the app:
  - **Theme:** **System default** follows your phone. **Light** and **Dark** pin it.
  - **Accent color:** the color that tints the app and your home-screen widgets.
    **Automatic** (the default) follows your device's Material You wallpaper colors.
    You can also pick **KashCal Teal**, KashCal's own color, or one of 11 other
    swatches. **More colors** opens wheels with 92 named colors. KashCal Teal is also
    the fallback when Material You isn't available. The hub avatar, the **Accounts &
    Settings** button and today's date in the calendar grid keep a thin outline, so
    they stay visible with a pale or dark accent.
  - **App Icon:** keep **Default**, or switch to the **Supporter** icon, a gold card
    with a heart, to show you've chipped in (or
    [would like to](https://kashcal.onekash.org/donate)). One version keeps the KashCal
    name. The other, **Supporter, labeled Calendar**, shows the name Calendar on your
    home screen. Changing the icon can restart the app. Notifications always use the
    default icon and name.
  - **Widget theme** and **Widget accent:** your
    [home-screen widgets](../features/widgets.md) can follow the app or have their
    own look. Leave both on **Follow app**, or pin the widgets to Light or Dark and
    give them their own color. See
    [Widgets](../features/widgets.md#colors-and-theme-together-or-apart).
- **Own your calendar**, the things you do with your schedule:
  - **[Invites](../events/invites.md)**: every invitation waiting on your reply. When
    one is waiting, a count shows on the row and on the top-bar avatar.
  - **[Recent changes](../sync/how-sync-works.md#recent-changes)**: what sync changed in
    your iCloud and CalDAV calendars over the last 7 days.
  - **Go to date**: jump to any date in the calendar.
  - **[Share availability](../features/share-availability.md)**: send someone your
    free times as plain text.
  - **[Manage tags](../events/tags.md)**: recolor, rename and remove the tags you've
    used across your events.
- **Privacy & Security**, who can see your calendar and what it can reach:
  - **[App lock](../features/app-lock.md)**: a switch that makes KashCal ask for a
    strong biometric, such as your fingerprint, or your screen lock before it opens.
  - **App permissions**: one screen listing every permission KashCal uses, each with
    the reason it needs it and a link to its system setting.
  - **How your data stays yours**: opens the [Privacy & Security](../privacy/overview.md)
    page in your browser.
- **About**: the version and app info.

## App shortcuts

Long-press the KashCal icon on your home screen to jump to a task:

- **New Event** opens the event form, starting at the next hour today. With
  [Smart event add](../events/smart-event-add.md) on, it opens the Smart event add
  box instead.
- **Today** opens KashCal on today's date.
- **Search** opens search.

## Quick Settings tile

KashCal has an **Add event** tile for Android's Quick Settings panel. Add it by
editing your Quick Settings tiles. Tapping it opens KashCal the same way as the
**New Event** shortcut, from any screen.

## Searching

Open search from the magnifier in the top bar or the **Search** app shortcut. With no
filter chip picked, search shows upcoming events only. Pick **All**, **Week**,
**Month** or **Date** (a day or a range) to change that. See
[Search](../features/search.md).

## Syncing on demand

Pull down on a calendar view to sync your connected accounts right away. This works
when you're online and have an iCloud or CalDAV account set up. KashCal also syncs in
the background on a schedule you set. See [How sync works](../sync/how-sync-works.md).
