# Navigation & shortcuts

Getting where you want to go takes a tap or a swipe. Here are the small moves that
keep KashCal out of your way: the drawer, home-screen shortcuts, search, and an
on-demand sync.

## The navigation drawer

Swipe from the left edge of the screen, or tap the menu icon, to open the drawer.
From here you can:

- **Switch views:** Month, Agenda, Day, 3 Days, Week, Month (Full), Year, and Insights.
- **Show or hide calendars.** Each calendar has a checkbox and a color dot. Your
  calendars are grouped by account, with separate sections for **Contacts**
  (birthdays and anniversaries) and **Device calendars** (calendars from other apps
  on your phone).
- **Open Settings**.
- **Share feedback**.

## App shortcuts

Long-press the KashCal icon on your home screen to jump straight to a task:

- **New Event** opens the event form right away.
- **Today** opens KashCal on today's date.
- **Search** opens search immediately.

## Searching

Open search from the top bar or the **Search** app shortcut. You can filter results
by date: this week, this month, all events, or a custom range. See
[Search](../features/search.md).

## Syncing on demand

Pull down on any calendar view to trigger an immediate sync with your connected
servers. KashCal also syncs in the background on a schedule you control. See
[How sync works](../sync/how-sync-works.md).
