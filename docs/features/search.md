# Search

Search finds events by their words when you can't remember the date. Tap the search
icon in the top bar, or long-press the KashCal app icon and choose **Search**.

## What it searches

Search looks through event titles, locations, notes, and guest names and email
addresses, in every calendar KashCal shows, [device calendars](../sync/device-calendars.md)
included. Results appear as you type, once a word has two characters. In Chinese, Japanese
and Korean, one character is enough.

- A word matches anywhere and in any case: "tist" finds "Dentist". Accents count:
  "cafe" doesn't find "Café".
- More words narrow the results. Every word has to appear in the event.
- Put words in quotes to find them together, in that order: "team meeting" finds
  "Team meeting" but not "Meeting with the team".

Matched words are highlighted. When the title doesn't explain a result, it shows where
the match is, after **Location:**, **Notes:** or **Guest:**. Each result also says how far
away it is, such as "in 3 weeks" or "2 months ago".

## Upcoming and past

Search opens on **Upcoming**. Tap **Past** for events that have ended. Both show
their count.

- **Upcoming** lists the soonest first, including events in progress.
- **Past** lists the most recent first, with a year heading when results span years.
- A repeating event shows once in each list, with its next date ("Next:") or its last
  date ("Last:").

If nothing is upcoming, you see "No upcoming events", with a button to show the past
matches when there are any.

## Filter by date

Tap **Date** and pick **This week**, **This month** or **Pick dates…**. To pick dates,
tap a day, then tap it again for that day alone, or tap another day for the range
between them. Tap **Upcoming** or **Past** to go back.

## Hidden calendars and no results

Events in calendars you've hidden in the navigation drawer aren't listed, but search
counts them, such as "3 more in hidden calendars". Tap **Show** to list them until you
close search; it doesn't change which calendars are visible. Tap **Leave out** to hide
them again.

If nothing matches, you see "No events match" and your search. When your
[Sync lookback](settings.md#sync) isn't **All events**, the message also shows it, with a
**Sync settings** button to change it.

Closing search clears the query and the filters.
