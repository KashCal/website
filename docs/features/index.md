# All features

Everything KashCal can do, in one place. Tap into any feature for the details.

## Viewing your calendar

- **[Calendar views](../calendar/views.md)**: month, agenda, day, 3-day, week, full-month, and year, with pinch-to-zoom and drag-to-reschedule.
- **[Navigation & shortcuts](../calendar/navigation.md)**: the drawer, home-screen shortcuts, and getting around fast.
- **[Widgets](./widgets.md)**: agenda, week, month, date, and upcoming widgets for your home screen.

## Creating events

- **[Quick Add](../events/quick-add.md)**: type events in plain language.
- **[The event form](../events/event-form.md)**: full control over every detail.
- **[Recurring events](../events/recurring.md)**: repeating events and editing scopes.
- **[Reminders](../events/reminders.md)**: alerts that survive reboots.
- **[Event colors](../events/colors.md)**: color-code your schedule.
- **[Scheduling & invitations](../events/attendees.md)**: invite people, send meeting invitations, and RSVP.
- **[Share an event as a card](./share-as-card.md)**: send an event as an image with a tappable calendar file.

## Connecting calendars

- **[Sync & accounts](../sync/index.md)**: iCloud, CalDAV, feeds, and device calendars.
- **[Supported servers](../sync/supported-servers.md)**: the full compatibility list.

## Getting more from your time

- **[Search](./search.md)**: full-text search with date filters.
- **[Insights](./insights.md)**: on-device analytics about how you spend your time.
- **[Contact birthdays](./birthdays.md)**: birthdays and anniversaries from your contacts.
- **[Backup & restore](./backup-restore.md)**: save and restore your settings.
- **[App lock](./app-lock.md)**: keep your calendar private behind a lock.
