# All features

Every KashCal feature, with a link to its page.

## Viewing your calendar

- **[Calendar views](../calendar/views.md)**: month, agenda, day, 3-day, week, planner, full-month, and year, with pinch-to-zoom and drag-to-reschedule.
- **[Navigation & shortcuts](../calendar/navigation.md)**: the drawer, the account hub, and home-screen shortcuts.
- **[Widgets](./widgets.md)**: agenda, week, month, date, and upcoming widgets for your home screen.

## Creating events

- **[Smart event add](../events/smart-event-add.md)**: type an event in plain language.
- **[The event form](../events/event-form.md)**: every detail of an event on one screen, including its own time zone.
- **[Recurring events](../events/recurring.md)**: repeating events and editing scopes.
- **[Reminders](../events/reminders.md)**: alerts that still go off after you restart your phone.
- **[Event colors](../events/colors.md)**: color-code your schedule.
- **[Tags](../events/tags.md)**: label events by what they are, and manage tag colors and names in one place.
- **[Scheduling & invitations](../events/attendees.md)**: invite people, send meeting invitations, and RSVP.
- **[Invites](../events/invites.md)**: every invitation waiting on your reply, in one list.
- **[Share an event as a card](./share-as-card.md)**: send an event as an image with a calendar file attached.

## Connecting calendars

- **[Offline calendars](../sync/offline-calendars.md)**: calendars on your phone that work with no account. Start with Local and add, rename, recolor or delete more.
- **[Sync & accounts](../sync/index.md)**: iCloud, CalDAV, feeds, and device calendars.
- **[Supported servers](../sync/supported-servers.md)**: the full compatibility list.
- **[Device calendars](../sync/device-calendars.md)**: show and edit the calendars already on your phone, such as Google or Outlook.
- **[Calendar feeds](../sync/ics-subscriptions.md)**: subscribe to ICS feeds, or add a holiday calendar from the built-in list.
- **[Import & export](../sync/import-export.md)**: open .ics files, and export any calendar KashCal keeps on the phone, or a single event.
- **[Contact sync](../sync/contacts.md)**: sync contacts with your account both ways, including photos (beta).

KashCal also opens .ics files and webcal:// links, and handles "Add to calendar" from other apps.

## Getting more from your time

- **[Search](./search.md)**: search titles, locations, and notes, with date filters.
- **[Insights](./insights.md)**: on-device figures on how you spend your time.
- **[Share availability](./share-availability.md)**: send someone your free times as plain text.
- **[Contact birthdays](./birthdays.md)**: birthdays and anniversaries from your contacts.

## Settings and privacy

- **[Settings](./settings.md)**: every setting and its default.
- **[Backup & restore](./backup-restore.md)**: back up your offline calendars to a folder, and save your settings to a file and restore them.
- **[App lock](./app-lock.md)**: hide your calendar behind your fingerprint, face, or screen lock.
- **[Languages](./languages.md)**: English plus 67 translations.
- **[Privacy & Security](../privacy/overview.md)**: no tracking, no accounts, no KashCal servers.
