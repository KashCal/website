# Home-screen widgets

The best calendar is the one you don't have to open. KashCal has five home-screen
widgets that keep your day in view. Add them the usual Android way: long-press your
home screen, choose **Widgets**, and find KashCal.

<WidgetMock />

| Widget | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Today's Agenda** | Today's events in a list, with past events dimmed and upcoming ones highlighted. Has an "add event" button, and says so when your day is clear. |
| **Week View** | The next seven days at a glance. |
| **Month View** | A full month grid with colored dots marking days that have events. Single-letter day names run across the top, today is marked with a filled circle, and you can add a week-number column. Page through months with the ‹ › arrows, tap the title to jump back to the current month, and tap **+** to add an event, all without opening the app. |
| **Date** | Today's date, styled like an app icon, for a clear at-a-glance day marker. |
| **Upcoming** | The next ten days of events, skipping days with nothing on them. |

Tap any event on a widget to open it in KashCal, and use the **+** button on the
agenda, month, and upcoming widgets to add one.

The month widget follows two of your settings live: turn on **Show week numbers** or
change **Start week on**, and it updates without a restart.

:::tip[Pick the right widget from the picker]
On Android 15 and newer, each widget previews as itself in the system widget picker,
so the agenda shows a sample day, the month shows a grid, and so on. You can tell them
apart before you place one.
:::

## Keeping widgets current

Widgets refresh when you add, change, or delete events, after a sync, and at the
start of each new day, so they stay accurate without you doing anything.

## Colors and theme, together or apart

By default your widgets wear whatever the app wears. But a calendar on your wall can
read differently from the one in your hand, so the widgets also have a look of their
own. Both live in the [account hub](../calendar/navigation.md#the-account-hub), under
**Make it yours**:

- **Widget theme:** leave it on **Follow app** and each widget tracks the app's light
  or dark face; pin it to **Light** or **Dark** and it holds that face on your home
  screen no matter what the app or your phone is doing.
- **Widget accent:** leave it on **Follow app** to match the app's accent, choose
  **Automatic** to follow your Material You wallpaper colors on Android 12 and newer, or
  pick one of the 92 colors for the widgets alone. Every widget recolors to match, add
  button and all.

## Two lines, or one

By default a list widget fits each event on a single line: a color, a start time, and
the title. When you also want to see when things *end*, turn on **Detailed widget
rows** in Settings, under **Appearance**. Each event grows to two lines, the title on top
and its full start-to-end time below, so the shape of your day reads from the home
screen. The time follows your in-app 12- or 24-hour setting. This applies to the
agenda, week, and upcoming widgets; leave it off and rows stay compact.

## How many events show

For the agenda-style widgets you can set a **Widget event limit** in Settings, under
**Appearance**, to control how many events appear per day. The default is 5.
