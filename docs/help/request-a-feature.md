# Requesting a feature

Good ideas are always welcome. Here's how to share one in a way that's most likely
to land.

## First, a quick check

- [ ] I've confirmed KashCal doesn't already do this (browse the [features](../features/index.md)).
- [ ] I've checked [Known limitations](./known-limitations.md) in case it's a deliberate choice.
- [ ] I've searched [existing issues](https://github.com/KashCal/KashCal/issues) for the same idea. If it exists, add a 👍 or a comment rather than opening a duplicate.

## What makes a good request

- **The problem, not just the solution.** Describe what you're trying to accomplish
  and why. That often leads to a better answer than a specific implementation idea.
- **A concrete example.** "When I do X, I'd like Y to happen" is much easier to act on
  than a general wish.
- **How often it affects you**, and whether there's a workaround today.

## Where to suggest it

Open an issue on the
**[KashCal GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/KashCal/KashCal/issues)** and
label it as a feature request (or describe it as one).

## A note on scope

KashCal aims to be a focused, fast, private calendar, not to do everything. Some
ideas may be declined to keep the app coherent and maintainable, even if they're
good. That's not a rejection of you; it's how the project stays healthy. Thanks for
understanding, and for caring enough to suggest improvements.
