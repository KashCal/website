# Requesting a feature

Ideas are welcome. Here's how to share one so it's most likely to land.

## First, a quick check

- [ ] I've confirmed KashCal doesn't do this already (browse the [features](../features/index.md)).
- [ ] I've checked [Known limitations](./known-limitations.md) in case it's a deliberate choice.
- [ ] I've searched [existing issues](https://github.com/KashCal/KashCal/issues) for the same idea. If it exists, add a 👍 or a comment instead of opening a duplicate.

## What makes a good request

- The problem, not only the solution. Describe what you're trying to do and why. That
  often leads to a better answer than a specific implementation idea.
- A concrete example. "When I do X, I'd like Y to happen" is easier to act on than a
  general wish.
- How often it affects you, and whether you have a workaround.

## Where to suggest it

Open an issue on the
[KashCal GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/KashCal/KashCal/issues) and choose
the Feature Request form. It asks for the problem, your proposed solution, the area of
the app and any alternatives you considered.

## A note on scope

KashCal aims to be a focused, fast, private calendar, not to do everything. Some good
ideas get declined to keep the app coherent and maintainable. That's not a rejection
of you; it's how the project stays healthy. Thanks for understanding, and for caring
enough to suggest improvements.
