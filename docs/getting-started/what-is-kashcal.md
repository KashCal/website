# What is KashCal?

Your days are scattered across a handful of calendars. Family plans on iCloud, work on
a CalDAV server, holidays from a link you subscribed to once, birthdays in your
contacts. KashCal puts them on one screen and stays out of your way.

It's a free, open-source calendar for Android: fast to open, easy on the eyes, and
serious about the small stuff.

And it's private by default. There's no account to create, no analytics, no
tracking and no KashCal server. KashCal syncs your events only with the calendar
accounts *you* connect. See [Privacy & Security](../privacy/overview.md) for
everything that leaves your phone.

## What you can do with it

- See your schedule seven ways: Agenda, Day, 3 Days, Week, Month, Month (Full) and Year.
- Sync with the services you already use: iCloud, Nextcloud, Fastmail, Radicale, Baikal, Zoho, mailbox.org, Stalwart, SOGo, and any standard CalDAV server.
- [Show your phone's other calendars](../sync/device-calendars.md), like Google or Outlook, once you turn on Device calendars.
- Add events by typing. With Smart event add on, "Coffee with Kash tomorrow 3pm" becomes an event.
- Get reminded. Reminders survive a phone restart, and you can snooze or dismiss them.
- Subscribe to calendar feeds: holidays, sports schedules and school terms from ICS links.
- Show birthdays and anniversaries from contacts as yearly all-day events.
- [Sync contacts with your account both ways](../sync/contacts.md), including photos, from iCloud and other CardDAV servers (beta).
- [Label events with tags](../events/tags.md) and manage their colors and names in one place.
- Put your day on the home screen: agenda, week, month, date and upcoming widgets.
- Search titles, locations and notes with full-text search.
- See where your time goes with Insights, worked out on your phone.
- Use it in your language: 67 languages, following your device setting.

## Who it's for

Anyone whose calendars live in more than one place, and anyone who'd rather their
calendar app didn't have an account, an ad, or an opinion about their data.

## Requirements

KashCal runs on Android 12 and newer.
