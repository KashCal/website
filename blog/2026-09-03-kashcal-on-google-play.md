People kept asking if KashCal was on Google Play. Now it is.

{/* truncate */}

It's the same app that's been on F-Droid and GitHub. Same [privacy](/docs/privacy/overview), too: no account, no ads, nothing sent to us.

Mostly, we want to say thanks. To the people who installed KashCal early, reported the bugs we swore weren't there, sent patches, and [donated](/donate) to keep it going.

Get it on Google Play, or [anywhere else](/docs/getting-started/install) you'd rather.
