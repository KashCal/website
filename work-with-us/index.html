The build-vs-buy math

If you've scoped a native Android calendar, you already know what it costs.

Build it yourself

What you get

  • Your brand. App name, icon, color palette, splash, and marketing copy. Swapped at build time, not bolted on at runtime.
  • Your defaults. Pre-configured CalDAV host, account hints, support links, deep-link domains. The customer's first launch already feels like yours.
  • Sync that's been beaten in production. iCloud, Nextcloud, Fastmail, Radicale, Baikal, Zoho, SoGo, Stalwart, and the rest of the CalDAV long tail. RFC 5545, 4791, 6578, and 7986 compliance, tested against real servers.
