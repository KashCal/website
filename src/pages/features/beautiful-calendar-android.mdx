## Modern, without the air quotes

KashCal is built on Material 3 with full **Material You** support, so on Android 12 and newer it picks up the colors from your wallpaper and wears them without looking like it's trying. The app runs edge-to-edge instead of boxed in by status bars. Dark mode is designed dark, not inverted light. Scroll, pinch, swipe, and the whole thing moves the way your phone does, not like a port of something older.

## Colors

> Couldn't pick a color? We couldn't either. So we added all 92.

Twelve curated colors in the primary grid for quick picks, plus 92 named CSS3 colors on a hue wheel for when the mood is specifically *dodgerblue*. Pick a color for any event and it rides along wherever the event goes, so apps that render per-event colors, like Apple Calendar on the Mac, show the same one.

## Common questions

**Does KashCal support Material You?**

Yes. On Android 12 and newer, KashCal picks up colors from your wallpaper using Material You dynamic color. Widgets match. Light and dark follow your system setting.

**How many colors can I use for events?**

Twelve curated colors in the primary grid for quick picks, plus 92 named CSS3 colors on a hue wheel when you want something specific. Pick a color for any event, and it rides along wherever the event goes, so apps that render per-event colors, like Apple Calendar on the Mac, show the same one.

**Does KashCal have a dark mode?**

Yes, and it is designed dark, not inverted colors. KashCal follows your Android system light/dark setting by default.

**Is KashCal free?**

Yes. KashCal is free, open source, and has no ads, trackers, or accounts. It is distributed through F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid, and Obtainium.
