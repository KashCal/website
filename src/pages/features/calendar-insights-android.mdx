# See where your time goes

Most calendars show you the future. KashCal also shows you the past.

You already know your Tuesdays are bad. KashCal will tell you how bad, and which days are giving you a break, and where your next long stretch of open time is hiding. Without a tracker. Without logging anything. Your calendar is already the log.

<Screenshot src="/img/screenshots/Insights.png" alt="KashCal Insights weekly breakdown" align="right" caption="A quiet look at your week." />

## Aim it at whatever you want

Insights only looks at the calendars you have switched on. Turn off your work calendar in the nav drawer and you get a window on your personal time. Turn off personal and you see the work view. Leave everything on for the honest picture. Same feature, whichever question you're asking.

## Nothing leaves your phone

The math runs on your device. There's no KashCal server, no cloud, no account to create, no work email required. Free, offline, computed on the phone in your pocket.

## Every calendar you have switched on

Personal Google, iCloud, Nextcloud, Fastmail, Outlook, your phone's local calendar, an ICS feed of your kid's school year, whatever else is in your drawer. If it's switched on in KashCal, it counts. If it's off, it doesn't. You're the filter.

A calendar that remembers what you did, not just what's coming, turns out to be more useful than you'd guess.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does KashCal show me about my time?**

Which day of the week eats the most of your calendar, the longest run of back-to-back commitments, how many meeting-free days you have, how much of your weekend is already booked, how early your earliest and how late your latest, which calendar is taking up the most of your time, and when your next long stretch of open time is.

**Is my calendar data sent anywhere?**

No. Everything in Insights is computed on your phone. No KashCal server, no cloud analytics, no account. Your calendar never leaves your device for this feature.

**Do I need a work account?**

No. KashCal Insights works with any calendar you have switched on in the app: your personal Google account, iCloud, Nextcloud, your phone's local calendar, or an ICS feed. There is no paid tier and no Workspace requirement.

**Can I see insights for only my personal calendar, or only work?**

Yes. Insights only looks at calendars you have made visible in the navigation drawer. Turn off your work calendar and Insights shows your personal time. Turn off personal and you get the work view. Leave everything on for the full picture.
