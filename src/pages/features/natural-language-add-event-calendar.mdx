# Add events the way you'd say them

Most calendars make you fill in a form: a title here, a date picker there, a
separate screen for "repeat every." KashCal lets you type the whole thing in one
line, the way you'd say it out loud, and sorts out the rest. "Dinner with Kash
next Friday 7pm" becomes a real event, on the right day, at the right time,
before you've reached for a single dropdown.

As you type, a live preview shows what KashCal understood, so you watch it get
the date, time, and repeat right before you save.

## It shows its work

While you type, KashCal shows what it parsed, the title, the date as
Today/Tomorrow or a full date, the time range, the location, and any repeat.
If something looks off, keep editing and the preview updates instantly. Want
more control? Open the full event form to fine-tune anything.

## A few things you can type

| You type | KashCal creates |
|----------|-----------------|
| `Coffee with Kash tomorrow 3pm` | "Coffee with Kash", tomorrow at 3:00 PM |
| `Standup every weekday at 9am` | "Standup", repeating every weekday at 9:00 AM |
| `Rent last day of the month` | "Rent", on the last day of every month |
| `Team sync last Thursday of the month` | "Team sync", the last Thursday of each month |
| `Deep work 2pm #focus` | "Deep work", today at 2:00 PM, tagged #focus |
