# Add events the way you'd say them

Most calendars make you fill in a form: a title here, a date picker there, a
separate screen for "repeat every." With smart event add, you type the whole
thing in one line, the way you'd say it out loud. "Dinner with Kash next Friday
7pm" becomes an event on that Friday at 7:00 PM, without a single dropdown.

As you type, a live preview shows what KashCal understood, so you can check the
date, time and repeat before you save.

## Turn it on

Smart event add is off until you turn it on. Tap **Accounts & Settings** in the
[account hub](/docs/calendar/navigation#the-account-hub), then switch on **Smart event add** under **Event preferences**.
After that, the + button opens the typing box in every calendar view. The
[guide](/docs/events/smart-event-add#turn-it-on) has the details.

## It shows its work

While you type, the preview shows the title, the date (Today, Tomorrow,
Yesterday or the date), the time range, the location, the note, any repeat and
your tags. If something looks off, keep typing and the preview updates. For
more control, tap **More options** to open the full event form with what you
typed filled in.

## Share text into it

Share text from another app to KashCal, and the typing box opens with the text
filled in, even when smart event add is off. The first link in the text becomes
the location, unless the text names a place with "at". Text longer than 500
characters opens the full event form instead.

## A few things you can type

| You type | KashCal creates |
|----------|-----------------|
| `Coffee with Kash tomorrow 3pm` | "Coffee with Kash", tomorrow at 3:00 PM |
| `Standup every weekday at 9am` | "Standup", repeating every weekday at 9:00 AM |
| `Rent last day of every month` | "Rent", on the last day of every month |
| `Team sync last Thursday of the month` | "Team sync", the last Thursday of each month |
| `Deep work 2pm #focus` | "Deep work", 2:00 PM on the selected day, tagged #focus |

Without a date, the event goes on the day you have selected. In the Day, 3 Days
and Week views, it goes on today.

## Learn the full vocabulary

The docs list every phrase KashCal understands, with examples: dates, times,
durations, offsets, repeats, locations, tags and notes.
